# 🐉 Dragon Ball 2048 — Power Evolution

A Dragon Ball-inspired 2048 puzzle game where you merge characters, unlock transformations, and evolve your way toward **Mastered Ultra Instinct**.

Combine familiar fighters, discover stronger forms, and push your evolution as far as possible in this anime-inspired puzzle experience.

> A fan-made fusion of the classic 2048 puzzle and the Dragon Ball universe.

## 🎮 About the Game

**Dragon Ball 2048 — Power Evolution** brings the classic number-merging gameplay of 2048 into a Dragon Ball-inspired character evolution journey.

Merge identical characters to evolve them into stronger fighters and transformations. Every merge gets you closer to the ultimate forms.

- 🐉 Dragon Ball-inspired character evolution

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Start with the available character tiles on the board.
2. Move tiles using your controls.
3. Merge two identical tiles to evolve into the next fighter.
4. Continue merging characters to unlock stronger transformations.
5. Plan your moves carefully to avoid filling the board.
6. Reach the highest evolution possible!

### Controls

- ⬆️ Move Up
- ⬇️ Move Down
- ⬅️ Move Left
- ➡️ Move Right

Use keyboard controls on desktop and touch controls on compatible mobile devices.
